Christmas was almost over. The kids, aged five and seven, had already opened their presents and were waiting for Jane to open hers. Two big, heavy boxes weighing about fifteen pounds each.

The first box was cube-shaped, and Jane excitedly opened it—a gold bowling ball. She had taken up bowling and wanted her own ball. David had been given the hint to pass on to me that she wanted something gold. So perfect, I thought.

Next she opened the longer and heavier gift—an eight-foot length of inch-and-a-half rusty chain.

"What is this?" she demanded to know.

"David hinted to me you wanted a gold bowling ball and an old chain," I said.

"No!" David cried. "I said a gold chain!"

"No, you said she wanted something gold. So I got the bowling ball and an old chain."

David was more upset than Jane, protesting that he'd said gold chain.

Anyhow, it was a joke. She did get the gold chain later.
