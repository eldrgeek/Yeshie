My dad was a wonderful man, and one of the things that made him so wonderful was his predictability. An example was the way he took his shower.

Mom said that Dad grew up very poor, and as a result he would be very conservative with his use of water.

Typically, when Dad took a shower, he'd turn on the water and soak himself. Then off would go the water and he'd soap up. Then on would go the water, he'd rinse, and then be done.

To me it just seemed like he was setting me up for something that needed to be taken advantage of. My opportunity came one hot summer afternoon. Dad had finished his to-do list and went to take his shower. Upstairs he went and downstairs I went.

I heard the water go on and I waited. As expected, the water was turned off shortly thereafter. It was my chance to act.

In the basement were the control valves for the hot and cold water. How easy it was to turn them off and just wait.

It didn't take long—about five minutes—before I heard him calling.

"Sister! Sister! There is no water."

"I'll check it out," she answered. "There's no water down here either."

Slowly I turned the cold water on to a slow stream.

"Argh!" I heard. "Find Mark and tell him to turn on the G.D. water. Damn kid." I don't know how he knew I was involved. I did turn on the rest of the water, and Dad was able to finish his shower.
