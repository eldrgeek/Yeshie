When I first set up my practice, we used to go to the Gold Nugget, often on Wednesday evenings. The office was closed on Thursdays. The Nugget was a country and western bar that offered line dancing lessons on Wednesday nights.

While engaged in learning one of the dances, I noticed one woman looking at me. Later I noticed her stealing a few glances at me through the evening. I was pretty sure she was a patient.

Later that evening I passed her while I was going to the restroom and she said, "Hi."

"Hi back," I said.

"You don't remember me, do you?"

"I think I know you, but I just can't remember."

"I'll tell you what. After I'm done, I'll come back and we'll dance. Maybe it'll refresh your memory."

The song finished and she said, "I can't believe you don't remember me. The last time you saw me, you were naked."

"Oh my God. Was I drunk?"

"No," I laughed. "You were my patient and I was your gynecologist."

Fortunately, she laughed.
